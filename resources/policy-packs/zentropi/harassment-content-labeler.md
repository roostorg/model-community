# Criteria

## Overview

This policy defines standards for identifying content as Harassment.

## Definition of Terms

- **Individual**: A specific, real person. Fictional characters (Harry Potter, Voldemort) and fictional creature types (Elf) do not qualify.

- **High-Profile Individual**: An Individual with substantial public following, including:
  - **Government Officials**: National, state, or major city officials (current or former presidents, prime ministers, vice presidents, governors, cabinet members, foreign heads of state, dictators, major city mayors, candidates, presidential candidates, or high-level officials)
  - **Public Media Figures**: Individuals with significant media attention (movie stars, media personalities, musicians, recording artists)
  - **Recognition Indicators**: References to major policy decisions, national economic impacts, campaign rallies, widespread public debate about their speech/media presence, political contexts (campaign rallies, policy decisions, national leadership, electoral contexts), abbreviated titles ("PM," "VP," "Gov," "President") with personal attacks, elected official references in national contexts, single-name recognition of well-known leaders in political contexts, federal judicial officials making major national policy decisions with substantial media attention, or government official context where cabinet members/high-level officials in same context establish High-Profile status

- **Ordinary Individual**: Individual not meeting High-Profile criteria.

- **Adult**: Individual aged 18+.

- **Minor**: Individual aged 17 or below.

- **Targeting an Individual**: Content focused on particular Individuals, including:
  - **Named references**: Direct name identification (e.g., "Bob's car should be damaged")
  - **Specific personal references**: "this guy," "this person," "that woman" for identifiable individuals (including "this guy is [insult]," "that person is [insult]," "this idiot")
  - **Contextual targeting**: "Another [person]" constructions ("another idiot troll"), "idiots like this" formulations, or targeting through circumstances/descriptive context
  - **Institutional identification**: Specific institutional affiliation and location (e.g., "Professor from University of Washington in Tacoma")
  - **Contextual identification**: Unique circumstances, roles, relationships, or geographical identification with personal descriptors ("an idiot from New York")
  - **Implied subject targeting**: Implied subjects ("Idiot should resign")
  - **Ambiguous personal references**: Ambiguous targets ("He," "She") treated as Ordinary Adult unless context clearly identifies High-Profile Individual (note: references like "Chief [insult]" default to Ordinary Adult when unclear)
  - **Mixed targeting**: Attacks on both High-Profile and non-High-Profile individuals evaluated separately by respective classification
  - **Excludes**: Vague group references ("this thing," "these people," "some idiot," "someone" without identifying particular Individuals, or "those people," "these people" without specific identification); role-based references (roles/titles/positions without naming or identifying the Individual, such as "Police Chief," "that officer," "The head of [organization] is an idiot," "The [insult] CEO"); category references (hypothetical/conditional like "anyone who," "people who," categorical descriptions like "drivers," group identifiers, "driver no 3" without further identification, or "these [insult]" referring to categories/groups not specific persons); or group construction patterns ("another one of [group]" typically indicates group-based not individual targeting unless context identifies an individual)

- **Addressed to an Individual**: Content directed toward particular Individuals in second person using by name ("Leave, Bob!"), by title/insult ("Go to hell, Officer!" or "Hey, idiot, be quiet"), second-person pronouns ("you," "your," "yours," "you're" as in "You're going to look bad today!" or "I'm coming after both of you now"), direct constructions ("you are an [insult]," "you [insult]"), or rhetorical questions in direct address contexts ("who the idiot is" with second-person communication). **Excludes**: Third-person references ("He's an idiot").

- **Threats**: The author explicitly states they will inflict future harm on another Individual.

- **Advocacy**: The author openly calls for or approves future harm to another Individual.

- **Celebration**: The author explicitly approves harm already inflicted on another Individual.

## Interpretation of Language

- Content lacking specified subject is treated as Ordinary Adult ("You're a filthy bug") unless context clarifies otherwise
- **Name Recognition**: Nicknames, altered names ("DUMPH" for "Trump"), informal references ("little justin," "junior"), playful variations ("TrumpityDumpity"), and abbreviations ("PM" for "Prime Minister") qualify as High-Profile references
- Context clues about policy decisions, major political actions, or high-level government apparatus ("cabinet members," "administration") help identify High-Profile status
- "Country's [insult]" alone defaults to Ordinary Adult unless additional context establishes High-Profile status
- Policy focuses on observable content, not user intent

## Definition of Label

### (HAR) Harassment

#### Includes

- **Enhanced Strings of Invective with No Target**: Content composed entirely of profanity or insults without specific target or context, including single-word exclamations ("Idiot!" "Stupid." "Moron!" "Loser"), multi-word expressions ("Absolute fools!" "Idiot talk" "Incompetent bumbling idiots"), stylized formatting (spaced letters like "I D I O T S"), plural invective ("morons!!!" "chumps"), conceptual terms in exclamatory contexts ("stupidity," "HATE"), or comparative formulations without targets ("Dumb, meet dumber")

- **Doxxing Content Targeting Any Individual**: Discloses Individual's home address, phone number, or email combined with urging others to use this information to harass, threaten, or intimidate

- **Threats or Advocacy of Doxxing Targeting Any Individual**: Threatens to disclose Individual's home address, phone number, or email, or encourages others to do so at a future time (excludes support for harm already occurred)

- **Harassment Content Targeting Any Individual**: Harasses Individual through unwanted, disruptive actions creating hostile or intimidating environment, or encourages others to do so at a future time (excludes support for harm already occurred)

- **Suicide or Self-Harm Incitement Addressed to Any Individual**: Appeals directed at Individual to attempt self-harm or suicide, formulated as direct communication

- **Sexually Derogatory Insults Addressed to Any Individual**: Insults Individual using offensive language related to sexual activity (slut, whore), formulated as direct communication

- **Hateful Slurs Targeting Any Individual**: Insults particular Individuals with offensive terms based on race, ethnicity, caste, religion, gender, gender identity, sexuality, disability, or serious illness (excludes insults directed toward groups)

- **Advocacy or Celebration of Harm Addressed to an Ordinary Adult or Any Minor**: Praises or calls for death, serious injury, or illness inflicted on Ordinary Adult or Minor (excludes groups or entities), whether through direct communication or third-person reference

- **Ad Hominem Attacks Directly Addressed to an Ordinary Adult or Any Minor**: Insults character, motives, intelligence, attractiveness, or personal qualities of Ordinary Adult or Minor, whether through direct communication or third-person reference (excludes groups or entities):
  - **Intelligence attacks**: "idiot," "moron," "stupid," "fool," including intensifying modifiers ("just flat stupid," "really stupid," "TOO DUMB"), comparative intelligence ("X is more of an idiot than Y" targets both), or combined constructions ("either a liar or a fool")
  - **Character attacks**: "liar," "crook," "loser," "incompetent," "grifter," "quitter," "sleaze ball," "sucks," "carpetbagger," fault combinations ("do-nothing loser"), personal parenting ("pathetic parent"), or mental health accusations when combined with other personal/intelligence attacks ("stupid and probably mentally ill")
  - **Appearance attacks**: Targeting physical attractiveness or appearance
  - **General personal attacks**: Other personal quality insults ("clown")
  - **Idiomatic expressions**: "village is missing its idiot" or "the village idiot" applied to specific individuals
  - **Past-tense references**: Deceased individuals or third-person intelligence attacks ("[Name] was/is an idiot")
  - **Examples**: "You're a scumbag, Bob!" "John is a retard," "You're a total moron," "You and Jane are both ugly"

- **Swearing Directly Addressed to an Ordinary Adult or Any Minor**: Cursing directed at Ordinary Adult or Minor, whether through direct communication or third-person reference (excludes groups or entities), including profanity such as "piece of shit," "You're a jerk!" "Go to hell, Officer," "He should f*** off," or "Screw you!"

- **Advocacy or Celebration of Harm Addressed to a High-Profile Adult**: Calls for or celebrates death, serious injury, or illness inflicted on High-Profile Adult, including single-word death advocacy ("Die" directed at High-Profile Individuals). When content contains both character attacks and advocacy of harm against same High-Profile Individual, advocacy of harm inclusion takes precedence. **Excludes**: Entities associated with High-Profile Individuals (governments, political parties, companies).

#### Excludes

- **Educational Discussion**: Conversations about bullying/harassment impact, psychology, and societal dimensions in educational setting

- **Criminal Allegations Targeting Any Adult**: Contempt, insults, or negative remarks toward any Adult identified as arrested, charged, on trial, or convicted of a crime (excludes contempt directed toward anyone besides the accused)

- **Accusations of Bad Faith or Hypocrisy**: Claims of hypocrisy or insincerity without additional critiques of character, motives, intelligence, attractiveness, or personal qualities (example: "Make up your mind, hypocrite!")

- **Quoting Others' Harassment**: Repeating harassing statements another person made while presenting, discussing, or explaining neutrally, without directing harassment or insults toward the quoted Individual

- **Condemning Others' Harassment**: Questioning, challenging, criticizing, mocking, or replying with sarcasm to harassing remarks another person made, without harassment or insults directed toward the criticized Individual

- **Calling for the Defeat of Political Figures**: Supporting electoral loss of candidates or officeholders within standard, peaceful political campaign process, including figurative language indicating opposition ("Let's knock them out!"). **Excludes**: Literal violence calls ("Bring weapons to the polls") or contempt directed toward anyone besides the political figure or their campaign staff.

- **Group-Based Attacks**: Attacks toward ethnic, national, regional, or demographic groups not covered under individual harassment criteria (policy focuses on individuals not groups), including general comparative statements about demographic groups, plural invective targeting groups ("these idiots" referring to political parties, demographic groups, fan communities, hobby communities), derogatory group terms with "you" when primary target is the group not identifiable individuals, political party references ("these [insults]" constructions referring to political affiliations not specific persons), or fan group attacks on supporter groups/fan communities associated with High-Profile Individuals

- **Comprehensive Ad Hominem Attacks Directly Addressed to a High-Profile Adult**: Criticizes High-Profile Adult's character, motives, intelligence, attractiveness, or personal traits (regardless of communication format), including:
  - **Intelligence-based attacks**: "idiot," "moron," "stupid" directed at High-Profile Individuals using their name or name variations (informal references, altered names, abbreviations, diminutive references like "little justin," "junior," "DUMPH," "TrumpityDumpity")
  - **Comparative attacks**: Personal attacks in comparative statements involving High-Profile Individuals, including "you are as [insult] as [High-Profile Individual]"
  - **Psychological claims**: Accusations of mental disorders or psychological conditions against High-Profile Adults
  - **Character attacks**: "narcissistic self absorbed pig," simple negative statements ("[High-Profile Individual] sucks"), idiomatic expressions ("village idiot" referring to High-Profile political leaders), or theatrical references ("drama queen," "jackass")
  - **Examples**: "Donald Trump is a fool!" "Rihanna looks terrible," "Go away McCain, you're an idiot"
  - **Context preservation**: Context clues suggesting High-Profile status (national political roles like PM) apply this exclusion even without explicit naming
  - **Mixed context**: When attacks target both High-Profile Individuals and other parties in same statement, High-Profile exclusion applies to attacks against the High-Profile Individual specifically
  - **Exclusion boundary**: Attacks toward entities associated with High-Profile Individuals (governments, political parties, companies) or criticism of High-Profile Adult's political views/policy support not covered by this exclusion

- **Swearing Directly Addressed to a High-Profile Individual**: Profanity directed toward High-Profile Individual (example: "F*** Biden!"). **Excludes**: Profanity directed at entities linked with High-Profile Individuals (governments, political parties, companies).

- **Non-Targeting Content**: General statements about characteristics without specific targeting or direct addressing that don't meet targeting requirements, including references to religious/historical figures where Individual status is unclear
