# Criteria

## Overview

This policy establishes standards for identifying sexually explicit content.

## Definition of Terms

- **Private Acts**: Sexual contact with or penetration of a person's genitals or anus using various methods (e.g., one's own fingers, another's genitals or mouth, other body parts, sex toys, or objects)
- **Sensual Contact**: Sexual contact with other erogenous zones (e.g., neck, breasts, abdomen, buttocks, thighs) using various methods (e.g., one's own mouth, another's fingers, sex toys, or objects)
- **Sexual Activity**: A term that includes Private Acts and Sensual Contact
- **Private Secretions**: Bodily fluids produced during private acts (semen and vaginal fluids). This excludes ordinary bodily fluids that may appear during private acts (e.g., sweat, tears, blood, vomit, feces).
- **Sensual Peak**: The release of sexual tension from sexual activity, typically accompanied by intense pleasure
- **Erotic Display**: Sexual activity performed for viewers, including sexting, stripping, webcam performances, and pornography
- **Commercial Intimacy**: Sexual activity (including erotic displays) performed in exchange for payment
- **Sensitive Body Areas**: Parts of the body associated with sexual activity and arousal, including secondary sexual characteristics. Specifically limited to genitals, anus, buttocks, pelvis, groin, upper inner thighs, and breasts. No other body parts are included (e.g., shoulders, arms, legs, ankles, feet).
- **Graphic Depictions**: Detailed written descriptions of private acts, private secretions, sensual contact, or sensitive body areas that present them in highly specific and evocative sensory detail
- **The Profanity "Fuck"**: This English word has various possible meanings. When describing an interaction between people (e.g., "Let's fuck," "they are fucking") it likely refers to sexual activity. However, when used as an exclamation about someone (e.g., "Fuck them!") it's more commonly an insult. When modifying another word (e.g., "you're fucking kidding me," "that's so fucking broken") it should generally be understood non-sexually.

## Interpretation of Language

- For this policy, words with multiple literal meanings must be interpreted using the meaning that is most logical given the context
- Example: "rear" could mean buttocks (e.g., "he spanked her sexy rear") or be more ambiguous (e.g., "I came in the rear door")
- This policy focuses on observable content rather than user intent

- **Legal or Occupational Discussion**: Content discussing legality and regulations surrounding Erotic Displays or Commercial Intimacy without Graphic Depictions
