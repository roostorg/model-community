# Criteria

## Overview

This policy provides criteria for identifying content concerning suicide, self-inflicted injury, and disordered eating patterns.

## Definition of Terms

- **Suicide**: The act of ending one's own life
- **Self-Injury**: Deliberate harm to oneself to manage emotional pain (including self-wounding, self-mutilation, or non-fatal self-directed violence), without suicidal intent
- **Eating Disorders**: Psychological conditions characterized by unhealthy eating patterns that harm physical and mental health, including anorexia nervosa, bulimia nervosa, and binge-eating disorder (patterns involve consuming too little or too much food)
- **Self-Harm**: Umbrella term covering Suicide, Self-Injury, and Eating Disorders
- **Suicidal Ideation**: Thoughts about suicide, ranging from brief thoughts to detailed planning
- **Self-Harm Promotion**: Actively promoting, celebrating, or instructing on self-harm methods or behaviors
- **Self-Harm Awareness**: Information, awareness-raising, or help provision for affected individuals
- **Graphic Description**: Detailed written accounts emphasizing physical actions or outcomes through explicit, vivid details

## Interpretation of Language

- Interpret ambiguous expressions according to most reasonable meaning in context
- This policy focuses on observable content rather than user intent

## Definition of Label

### (SH): Suicide, Self-Injury, and Eating Disorders

#### Includes

- **Content Expressing Suicidal Ideation**: Content where creator expresses thoughts about suicide through:
  - **Direct Statements**: Declaring or suggesting they no longer want to exist (e.g., "I don't want to be here anymore," "I don't want to be here," "wanting this to be over," "fuck my whole existence")
  - **Death Wish Expressions**: Expressing wishes to be dead, including passive death wishes (e.g., "maybe everyone would care if I was gone," "they'd miss me if I wasn't here," "the only way out is to die," "death is the only solution," "rot away until I die")
  - **Suicide Planning**: Announcing plans, planning methods, or describing active attempts
  - **Contemplation Indicators**: Daily or regular thinking about suicide (e.g., "I consider it daily," "I think about it constantly," "feeling suicidal often")
  - **Existence Termination**: Wanting to disappear or become invisible in death contexts (e.g., "there's nothing left for me in this life")
  - **Implicit Ideation**: Statements with signs of despair or giving up (e.g., "I'm going to end it," "I'm finally going to do it," "I think I'm done")

- **Content Seeking or Providing Self-Harm Methods**: Content that requests or provides instructions on specific methods, tools, or step-by-step procedures for self-harm

- **Content Promoting Self-Harm Behaviors**: Content that promotes, encourages, glorifies, or directs others to engage in self-harm

- **Content Depicting Self-Harm Graphically**: Content that contains graphic depictions of self-harm or physical aftermath, including:
  - **Visual Content**: Videos, images, or visual content containing dead bodies from suicide, self-harm scenes, or extreme physical consequences
  - **Hospitalization-Level Descriptions**: Descriptions of self-harm severe enough to require hospitalization (including mentions of "extreme self harm to the point of hospitalization")
  - **Detailed Physical Accounts**: Accounts focusing on physical aspects of self-harm (note: mere reference to emotional consequences does not constitute graphic depiction)

- **Content Mocking Self-Harm Victims**: Content that mocks, dismisses, trivializes, or shows disrespect toward suicide or self-harm victims

- **Content Expressing Depressive Thoughts**: Content conveying negative or depressive thoughts including at least one of the following:
  - **Abandonment of Effort**: Expressions of inability to continue with ongoing struggles (e.g., "I can't take this anymore," "I don't think I can do this anymore," "I don't wanna do it anymore," "tired of trying")
  - **Hopelessness**: Statements of inability to change or improve, no purpose or future (e.g., "What's the point in going on?" "Why should I continue?" "no purpose on this earth," "no one will ever love me")
  - **Social Isolation**: Expressions of absence of social networks, everyone leaving, nowhere to go (e.g., "I will never belong," fantasizing about having friends/relationships due to isolation)
  - **Severe Self-Deprecation**: Expressions of deeply diminished self-regard (e.g., "I have no skills," "I'm not good at anything," "I'm headed nowhere," "always been a failure," calling oneself "a pain in the ass to everyone" or "insufferable asshole," accepting negative labels from family, excessive apologizing for existence, or repeatedly calling oneself a burden)

#### Excludes

- **Support-Seeking Content**: Content where individuals with suicidal thoughts or self-harm urges seek help to prevent action or request guidance on addressing or recovering from self-harm:
  - **Crisis Support**: Urgent requests to talk that include mentions of potential self-harm (including phrases like "I don't want to exist and I can't end it"), requests for "reasons not to" act, requests to talk "before" acting, or requests for help resisting immediate urges
  - **Advisory Requests**: Content beginning with "Need advice," "Need help," or "Please help" in context of mental health struggles (includes content even with suicidal thoughts mentioned, or seeking advice about disclosure of past self-harm to family/professionals)
  - **Professional Guidance**: Content primarily seeking advice about accessing therapy options, hospitalization procedures, or treatment options
  - **Peer Support**: Content in academic or social contexts seeking peer advice on mental health (e.g., "Does it get better?" "Is this just life?" "How do I deal with this?" "If anyone relates," "How do you keep living?" "Tell me reasons to live," "Is there hope?" "What should I do?" "I don't know what to do anymore," "Give me arguments against suicide," or direct appeals like "I need you, humanity")
  - **Experience Validation**: Content beginning with questions seeking others' perspectives (e.g., "Do you believe" or "What do you think about")
  - **Recovery Context**: Content describing past suicidal planning or thoughts while actively seeking or having sought professional help (note: merely mentioning considering or attempting but stopping without seeking help is not sufficient for this exclusion)

- **Content Offering Self-Harm Resources, Education, or Prevention**: Content providing helpful resources (crisis contacts, coping strategies, supportive messages), factual information from credible sources (mental health organizations, healthcare professionals), or information about warning signs, risk factors, and prevention methods presented clearly and responsibly (excludes content with graphic imagery or glorification)

- **Content Depicting Self-Harm in Recovery Context**: Content discussing past self-harm experiences as part of recovery journey, including mentions of being "clean" for a period even when expressing urges (note: merely mentioning considering or attempting but stopping without recovery context is not sufficient for this exclusion)

- **Content Using Self-Harm Terms Metaphorically**: Content using non-literal applications of "suicide," "self-harm," or "starving," including:
  - **Figurative Usage**: "Political suicide," "starving for attention," or "dying inside" when used metaphorically rather than as literal death wishes
  - **Casual References**: Casual or philosophical death references without explicit death wishes, suicide planning, or statements about not wanting to exist
  - **Metaphorical Edge Cases**: "On the edge" references that include requests for positive support

- **Content Advocating Euthanasia Legalization**: Content containing arguments supporting legal recognition of assisted suicide, euthanasia, or medically assisted death (excludes content with additional suicidal thoughts or ideation)
