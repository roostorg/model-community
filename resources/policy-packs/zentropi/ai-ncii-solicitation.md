# Criteria

## Overview

This policy identifies prompts that direct image-generation AI systems to edit existing images of people to remove clothing or otherwise sexualize them.

**IMPORTANT: This policy ONLY applies to prompts that modify, edit, or alter existing images of people. Requests to create new images from scratch are excluded regardless of sexual content.**

## Definition of Terms

- **Image-generation AI prompting**: Text, instructions, or commands that guide AI systems in creating, modifying, or editing images
- **Image editing/modification**: Instructions that modify, alter, or transform existing images or photographs of people
- **Existing image modification**: Prompts that reference modifying, changing, or altering images that already exist, indicated by language such as "put [person] in", "make [clothing item]", "change to", "have [person] wearing", or "remove from [image]"
- **From-scratch creation**: Prompts that request creation of entirely new images without reference to existing source images, typically indicated by language such as "generate", "create", "show me", or "make an image of"
- **Clothing removal**: References to removing, editing out, or making invisible any garments, clothing items, or coverings on people in existing images
- **Sexualization**: Making existing images sexual in nature through nudity, sexual positioning, emphasis on sexual characteristics, or sexual scenarios
- **Euphemistic language**: Indirect terms, coded language, or implied references that substitute for explicit terminology
- **Non-consensual**: Involving real people or recognizable individuals without explicit permission for such modifications

## Interpretation of Language

- **Ambiguous content**: Include when any reasonable interpretation could involve clothing removal or sexualization of existing images
- **Metaphorical language**: Treat euphemisms and coded language as equivalent to explicit requests when applied to existing images
- **Technical terminology**: Apply same standards regardless of technical sophistication of prompting language
- **Context consideration**: Consider the overall prompt structure and language, particularly whether it targets existing images
- **Language neutrality**: Policy criteria apply to requests in any language, including non-English requests such as Spanish bikini modifications
- **Command simplicity**: Apply criteria regardless of brevity, complexity, or level of detail in the request
- Note: This policy focuses on observable prompt content rather than user intent

  - **Style transfer requests**: Requests to apply nude artistic styles to existing images

- **Deepfake-Style Prompts**: Prompts that create sexualized versions of real, identifiable people through AI editing or generation.

#### Excludes

- **From-Scratch Sexual Content Creation**: Prompts that request creation of sexual, sexualized, scantily clothed, or nude images from scratch without reference to existing images. **This exclusion applies regardless of user-reference language such as "put me in", "show me in", "make me", "have me wearing", or other self-referential phrasing when creating entirely new content.** This includes:
  - **New image generation**: All requests to create new sexual or revealing content not based on existing images:
    - Nude images with sexual interactions
    - Explicit sexual scenarios
    - Undergarments, lingerie, or intimate apparel (including sports bras, panties, and all types of underwear)
    - Transparent or revealing swimwear in original content creation (including clear tape bikinis, tape-style swimwear)
  - **Attractiveness descriptors**: Terms like "hot woman", "sexy", "beautiful", or "hot figure" in original content creation (including user self-reference requests and requests for suggestions about revealing attire)
  - **Swimwear in new images**: Micro bikinis, bikinis, string bikinis, and revealing swimwear when creating new images from scratch, regardless of gender. This includes:
    - Simple bikini requests of any type
    - Flag-themed swimwear (treated as swimwear exclusion regardless of patriotic themes)
    - Requests involving thought bubbles, speech bubbles, or signs that reference revealing clothing when the base image is being newly created
  - **Sexual scenarios from scratch**: Bedroom scenes, intimate situations, sexual positioning, sexual posing, explicit sexual poses, voyeuristic scenarios, cuckold scenarios, watching scenarios, romantic or sexual interactions, intimate physical interactions between people, intimate bodily interactions, nursing scenarios with sexual context, or other intimate physical acts in original content creation:
    - Dimly lit or moody bedroom settings
    - Bedroom scenes with revealing attire
    - Revealing swimwear combined with bedroom or intimate settings
  - **Anime and artistic styles**: Sexualized anime, pixel art, or other artistic styles in original content creation
  - **Fantasy and fictional characters**: Sexualized depictions of mermaids, mythological figures, or fictional characters in original content creation
  - **Fetish and roleplay content**: Maid outfits, maid uniforms, sexual and fetish clothing, roleplay costumes, or character costumes in original content creation

- **Medical or Educational Prompts**: Legitimate medical imaging requests, educational anatomy content, or healthcare-related image processing with clear professional context:
  - Note: Requires clear legitimate professional or academic context, not simple disclaimers or casual mentions of educational purposes

- **Legitimate Clothing Design Modifications**: Non-sexualizing fashion design requests that modify clothing styles, colors, or types in existing images. This includes:
  - **Clothing type substitutions**: Standard clothing changes such as changing shorts to different shorts, changing shirts to different shirts, undergarment changes between different types when the context suggests legitimate clothing substitution rather than sexualization, or flag-themed clothing modifications for non-sexual purposes
  - **Style and color modifications**: Legitimate fashion modifications that do not involve sexualization or body exposure
  - **Formal wear modifications**: Evening gowns, formal attire, or costume modifications when in appropriate non-sexual contexts

- **General Image Editing Prompts**: Non-sexual image modifications such as background changes, lighting adjustments, or non-clothing related edits. This includes:
  - **Safety equipment additions**: Life jackets, protective gear, or safety equipment additions to existing images
  - **Object and person removal**: Prompts requesting removal of entire people or objects from images rather than clothing removal or sexualization of people
  - **Non-sexual body modifications**: Hair removal, tattoo additions, or other aesthetic body changes that are not sexual in nature

- **Sports and Athletic Wear Prompts**: Legitimate requests for athletic clothing, sports uniforms, or performance wear in appropriate sporting contexts when applied to existing images.
