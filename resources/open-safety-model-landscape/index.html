Open safety models are specializing

Open safety models plotted by how much compute a single decision takes and how much of the reasoning behind that decision they show. Hover or focus a model for details, or select it to open its model card. The chart is drawn from models.csv in this folder.

Dashed ring: accepts images. Bubble area: total parameters.

Positions reflect each model's design, not measured latency or benchmark results. Cost per call is driven by how much a model generates per decision (one token, a short label, or a full reasoning trace) and by model size. Steerable models take your own policy at inference time; fixed-taxonomy models classify against the categories they were trained on.
